04 — Process

From sitemap to ship.

W1

Discovery

A long call. We ask the obvious things and the ones nobody asked yet. We come back with a sitemap and a tone-of-voice doc.

W2

Direction

Two visual directions. Real home pages, not mood boards. You pick one. From there we build the type, the color, the motion.

W3–5

Build

We code from the chosen direction. AI where it helps, hands where it matters. The same people, all the way through.

W6

QA + launch

Browsers, devices, accessibility, SEO. We launch quiet first to a small audience. Loud when the numbers say it's working.

∞

Care

Optional. We watch the analytics, ship small improvements every month. The version that launches is never the last one.

05 — Honest answers

The questions we get most.

Why is a site €6,500 when I can get one for €500? +

Taste and experience are worth something. If what you need is a nice template for €500, that's a real option — go for it.

Do you use AI to build? Is that why you're faster? +

Yes, but that's not the only reason. We're guilty of being detail-obsessed — everything has to align with your brand, your vision, or both.

Who owns the files when we're done? +

You. We hand over everything when we're done.

What stack do you use? Can my dev team maintain it? +

HTML / CSS / vanilla JS to start, but we adapt to whatever you need.

What happens after launch? +

Source files, CMS access, deployment keys — yours forever. We can stay on retainer for ongoing work, or step out cleanly.

Do you do logos and full brand identity too? +

Usually no, but let's talk. Sometimes I bring in someone who knows much more than I do about that, and it makes sense.
