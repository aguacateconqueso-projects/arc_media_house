04 — Process

From sitemap to ship.

W1

Discovery

A long call, the messy doc, the audit. We come back with a sitemap, a tone-of-voice document, and the questions nobody asked yet.

W2

Direction

Two visual directions presented as full home pages, not mood boards. We commit to one. From there: type system, color, motion principles, component sketches.

W3–5

Build

Coded from the chosen direction, not from a Figma export. AI-assisted where it speeds craft, hand-tuned everywhere it matters. Same hands, all the way through.

W6

QA + launch

Cross-browser, cross-device, accessibility, SEO. Soft launch to a small audience first. Hard launch when the analytics say the site is doing what it should.

∞

Care

Optional retainer. We watch the analytics, ship small improvements monthly, keep the site moving. The version that launches is never the final one.

06 — Honest answers

The questions we get most.

Why is a site €6,500 when I can get one for €500? +

Both prices are honest for what they deliver. €500 buys a template adapted to your content — fine for a side project or a CV. €6,500 buys a custom site, a typographic system, a tone of voice, and the same hands from first call to launch. If a template solves your problem, we'll happily point you to two studios that build templates well.

Do you use AI to build? Is that why you're faster? +

Yes, where it speeds craft — boilerplate, refactors, copy variants, browser testing. No, where craft is the work — type, motion, the line of copy that opens the page. Faster delivery comes from a small team that doesn't hand the file to a stranger, not from automation cutting corners.

Who owns the files when we're done? +

You. All of it. Source code, design files, CMS keys, hosting credentials, agent prompts. Handed off in a single transfer doc. No proprietary lock-in. If you want a different studio next year, we make sure they can pick up where we left off.

What stack do you use? Can my dev team maintain it? +

Default stack: HTML / CSS / vanilla JS for brand sites and landings, Next.js or Astro for SaaS marketing, Sanity or Contentful when a CMS is needed. We adapt to your team's stack on request — no religion about tools, just whatever your devs already maintain.

What happens after launch? +

Two paths. One: clean handoff and you take it from there — we're a phone call away if something breaks. Two: a monthly care retainer where we watch analytics, ship small improvements, refresh copy and motion as the company evolves. Most clients pick the second after a month or two.

Do you do logos and full brand identity too? +

On request, yes — typography, color system, voice document, basic brand book. We don't do mark design (the logo itself) as a stand-alone service; we partner with two specialists for that when a project needs one.
