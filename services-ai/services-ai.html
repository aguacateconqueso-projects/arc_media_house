From brief to first conversation.

Four weeks of work, one set of hands, one ongoing relationship.

W1

Listen + Frame

What does the agent need to solve. What it shouldn't touch. Sources of knowledge — documents, site, internal pages. System prompt v1.

W2

Make

Build, internal testing, retrieval setup if applicable. The agent starts holding conversations with us before it holds them with anyone else.

W3

Rehearsal

Real-case testing with the client. Tone calibration, edge cases, refusals. The agent learns what to say, and what to stop saying.

W4

Ship

Deploy on site, handoff of prompts, logs and credentials. The client team sees how to read conversations and where to ask for tuning.

∞

Tuning

Prompts get sharper with real use. Monthly or biweekly tuning, depending on tier. The agent gets better at sounding like the company over time.

07

Questions worth asking.

Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude directly?

Because a generic model doesn't know your company. It guesses. A custom agent has a system prompt that holds your voice, retrieval over your real documents, and rails on what it should and shouldn't say. The difference shows in the first three messages.

What happens with our data? GDPR?

Data stays where it should. We work with vendors that offer EU data residency and zero-retention modes when needed. No client data is used to train external models. Data Processing Agreement signed before kickoff if required.

Who owns the prompt?

You. Source files, system prompt, retrieval index, credentials — handed off at launch. If you decide to take maintenance elsewhere, you're not starting from zero.

What model do you use?

Vendor-agnostic. We pick per case based on cost, latency, reasoning quality and language coverage. Anthropic, OpenAI, open-weights — whatever fits the brief. The agent stays portable.

What if the agent gets it wrong?

It will, sometimes. That's why every agent ships with logs, refusal rails for sensitive questions, and a handoff path to a human. Tuning is monthly minimum. Mistakes are inputs, not failures — they sharpen the prompt.

What about post-launch?

Running costs covered in section 03 — Light, Standard or Heavy depending on volume. The agent gets better with use. Monthly tuning is part of the deal.
