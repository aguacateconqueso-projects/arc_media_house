AM

Adrian Mendoza

Founder · Director

Trained as a violinist and trumpet player, became a cinematographer by vocation, and a stage director by accident. Born in Venezuela, spent his formative years filming symphony orchestras across the world's great theatres — including La Scala — then nine years in Buenos Aires building opera productions from the inside, first as assistant stage director, then as executive producer. He arrived in the corporate world and found the sharpest contrast of his life: companies with history and real things to say, communicating them without craft or coherence — in the film, in the website, in the first reply a client receives. He built ARC to work across that whole layer. He brings the same attention you'd give an opening night: if the curtain goes up tomorrow, everything needs to be considered today.
